ABOUT

Daniel Goodwin

I am a follower of Jesus, a husband, and a storyteller with a passion for truth. Through my writing, I seek to reflect God’s light, the power of Scripture, and the beauty of redemption.

My writing blends timeless storytelling with biblical truth, aimed especially at young Christian men and families. Whether through adventure, parable, or children’s tales, my goal is to point people to Jesus and inspire a life of faith. I want readers to enjoy the journey and carry something meaningful with them after the final page.

For 22 years, I proudly served in the United States Air Force. That career represents more than two decades of professional dedication, responsibility, and service to our country—a chapter of my life I will always value. Following retirement, I began a new chapter at Liberty University, studying theology and preparing for continued service through ministry, teaching, and writing.

Alongside my studies, I teach the Bible and explore how its truths speak to everyday life. Those same questions find their way into my stories through characters learning courage, facing difficult choices, and discovering what faithfulness requires.

My wife, Becca, inspires some of my most personal stories. Away from the page, I enjoy history, music, classic cars, and figuring out how things work. A stubborn engine and a well-timed laugh both have their place.

Whatever I write, my purpose remains the same: by His grace and for His glory.

Thank you for visiting. May your heart be stirred toward hope and courage in Christ.

FAITH & SERVICE

What I Believe

I believe the Bible is the inspired and inerrant Word of God, trustworthy and authoritative in what it teaches. Its truth shapes how I understand the world, the stories I tell, and the life I seek to live.

Jesus Christ, the Son of God, died for our sins and rose bodily from the grave. Salvation is God’s gift of grace, received through faith in Him. My hope rests in His finished work, and my desire is to point others toward the forgiveness, new life, and eternal hope found in Jesus.

Faith In Action

Putting God’s will first, I seek to live out my faith through teaching, discipleship, and everyday service. In Sunday School and men’s Bible study, my goal is to help people understand what Scripture says and apply its truth. I have a particular heart for encouraging men to follow Christ faithfully in their homes, churches, and communities.

That same purpose shapes my writing. Through stories of courage, sacrifice, and redemption, I hope to encourage (and hopefully entertain) believers and point those searching for hope toward Jesus. I am still learning and growing, trusting God to make fruitful the work He places before me.
